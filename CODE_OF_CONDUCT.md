# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want RSOSTBTEST-pro to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment:

* being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
* giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
* focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
* showing empathy towards other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
* sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention;
* publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
* submitting harmful content (for example operational instructions for
  violence or weapons) under the guise of benchmark tasks;
* other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues, tasks and
submissions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through GitHub (for example
via a private security advisory if no other channel is listed). All reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
